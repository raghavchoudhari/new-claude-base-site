Capabilities

Six systems, one continuously updating twin.

Every module below writes into the same worker-level twin record, so safety, ergonomics, and workforce planning teams are finally looking at the same ground truth.

⌚

Wearable biometric ingestion

Chest-strap HR/HRV, wrist IMU, and smart-PPE thermal sensors stream over private 5G/CBRS at sub-20ms latency, with on-device edge filtering to strip noise before it hits the twin.

📍

UWB + RFID spatial twin

Sub-30cm indoor positioning maps every worker against restricted zones, forklift paths, and machine danger radii — triggering geofenced alerts before a collision-course event, not after.

🧠

Predictive fatigue & injury modeling

A biomechanics ML model trained on 40M+ shift-hours forecasts micro-sleep risk, cumulative strain, and near-miss probability 60–90 minutes ahead, per worker, per task.

🛠️

MES/PLC task context

OPC-UA and MQTT connectors pull live cycle times and machine states from your existing SCADA stack, so the twin knows *what* a worker is doing, not just how their body is responding.

🥽

AR-assisted intervention

Smart-glasses push real-time posture correction, break prompts, and step-by-step SOP overlays directly to the worker whose twin flagged the risk — closing the loop in seconds, not shift reports.

🔒

Privacy-partitioned data model

Biometric streams are pseudonymized at the edge and governed by worker-council-approved consent tiers; raw physiological data never leaves the plant's private network slice.

Deployment

From loading dock to live twin in four phases.

01

Network & sensor survey (Week 1)

Our RF engineers map coverage for a private 5G SA slice or CBRS overlay across your floor plan, and fit 30 pilot workers with wearables calibrated to their baseline vitals.

02

Twin calibration & MES integration (Weeks 2–3)

We connect OPC-UA/MQTT feeds from your PLCs and SCADA, and train the fatigue/ergonomics model on your specific task cycles, shift patterns, and ambient conditions.

03

Supervisor console go-live (Weeks 4–5)

EHS and line supervisors get real-time twin dashboards and mobile alerting; workers get opt-in access to their own twin data and AR-guided micro-break prompts.

04

Plant-wide scale-out (Week 6+)

We expand from the pilot line to full-shift, full-plant coverage, with a dedicated slice of network capacity reserved so twin traffic never competes with production-critical systems.
